Metadata description – indicators_Prague.py

File: indicators_Prague.py
Case Study Area: Prague
Milestone: MS21 – Climate hazard indicators
Language: Python
Libraries: xarray, xclim, NumPy
Input: Daily/hourly gridded temperature, relative humidity, and wind data (NetCDF)
Output: Climate indicators including universal thermal climate index, annual mean temperature, annual mean relative humidity, summer days, and hot days

Description:
This script implements climate indicators tailored for the Prague case study within the CARMINE-T2.4 project. It focuses on thermal stress and basic temperature/humidity metrics to support urban climate risk assessment and adaptation strategies.

Methodology:

Universal Thermal Climate Index (UTCI): Combines air temperature, relative humidity, and wind to assess human thermal comfort and heat stress.

Air Temperature (air_t): Aggregates daily temperature data to compute annual mean temperature for climate monitoring purposes.

Relative Humidity (relhum): Aggregates daily relative humidity to compute annual mean values.

Summer Days (SU): Counts days with maximum temperature above 25°C over a given frequency (annual by default).

Hot Days (TX35): Counts days exceeding 35°C, implemented as a wrapper over the SU indicator function.

Key features:

Efficient multi-dimensional data handling with xarray

Modular design for integration into CSA-wide indicator workflows

Supports both daily aggregation and annual summary indicators

Reproducible computation for urban climate impact assessment

Intended use:
The script is designed for technical users analyzing thermal stress and temperature extremes in Prague. It can be integrated into the CARMINE repository workflow for indicator computation, post-processing, and visualization, ensuring consistency with indicators computed across other CSAs.
